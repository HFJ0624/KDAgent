# iTrust Official Dataset Context

## Dataset collection boundary

iTrust states that its critical-infrastructure testbeds are operated with and without launched attacks and that collected data are made available to researchers. The public dataset page also states that datasets are supplied on an as-is basis. Therefore, RCA conclusions should be evaluated against the held-out labels supplied by the experiment pipeline, not inferred from a generic description of an attack.
